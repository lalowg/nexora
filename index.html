02Capabilities

Four capabilities. One nervous system.

Most AI products bolt features together. NEXORA grows them from a shared body, so an agent can see what perception saw, and reasoning can set automation in motion without an integration project. Keep scrolling and watch the same neurons re-wire for each job.

01 · Capability

Agents

Give it an outcome, not a script.

An agent colony breaks one intent into cooperating specialists — research, drafting, checking, filing — each with its own scoped mandate. They hand context to one another the way cells pass signals, and any one of them can be recalled.

Decomposition
One request becomes a visible plan of sub-tasks before anything runs.
Handoffs
Agents pass full context, so nothing is lost between steps.
Recall
Every agent carries a mandate you can narrow or revoke at once.

02 · Capability

Automation

Processes that keep moving when the world doesn't cooperate.

Automations run as a circulation loop rather than a brittle chain. When a step stalls, the flow routes around it; when a connected system changes, the loop proposes its own re-wiring and shows you exactly what moved.

Event-native
Flows start from real events, not polling schedules.
Self-routing
Stalled steps are bypassed and retried, with a full trace.
Change-aware
A renamed field or new API version triggers a proposal, not a silent failure.

03 · Capability

Perception

Senses for everything your organisation already produces.

Documents, screenshots, recordings and live telemetry enter through receptive shells. Each layer abstracts a little further — from pixels and waveforms to entities, intent and anomalies — until the signal is something the rest of the organism can act on.

Multimodal
Text, images, audio and streams arrive through one intake.
Layered
Raw signal is refined in stages you can inspect one by one.
Grounded
Every conclusion links back to the evidence it came from.

04 · Capability

Reasoning

Thinking you can audit — including the roads not taken.

Before it acts, the organism grows a deliberation tree: candidate plans branch, get compared and are pruned. The chosen path is kept beside its alternatives, so you can see why it decided — and where it was unsure.

Branching
Several plans are drafted and weighed before one is chosen.
Traceable
Each step records its inputs, assumptions and cost.
Calibrated
Low confidence hands the decision to a person, by design.

04Growth

It grows the way your organisation does.

No big-bang rollout. NEXORA starts as a seed around one workflow and earns every new branch.

  1. 01Stage

    Seed

    Connect one workflow and one source of truth. The organism starts small on purpose.

  2. 02Stage

    Sense

    It watches how work actually moves: the handoffs, the waiting, the exceptions nobody wrote down.

  3. 03Stage

    Learn

    Patterns become proposals. Your team decides what it may do alone and what it must ask about.

  4. 04Stage

    Branch

    New capabilities grow from proven ones — never from a blank slate, always from evidence.

  5. 05Stage

    Prune

    Paths that stop earning their keep are retired, so the system stays legible and cheap to run.

  6. 06Stage

    Mature

    A stable intelligence that keeps adapting — inside the boundaries you set and can redraw.

Seed → Mature

05Protocol

Alive, not unsupervised.

An intelligence that grows needs boundaries that grow with it. Four rules are written into the organism itself, not bolted on afterwards.

  1. P1

    Observable

    Every action leaves a trace you can read, replay and question.

  2. P2

    Bounded

    Permissions are membranes: scoped, visible, and revocable in one move.

  3. P3

    Reversible

    If the organism can't undo an action, it doesn't take it alone.

  4. P4

    Answerable

    Above the thresholds you set, a named person decides. Always.
